Blockly AI Assistant: Neon

Settings

Prints the program view the assistant would send now to the browser console.

Prints the assistant's task events to the browser console, as they would go to the server log.

Beginners hear extra help, like which blocks fit a question.

While the assistant works, keys and the mouse don't change your program. While it waits for your answer, you can look around but not edit.

Navigation speech is who speaks your moves and messages; the assistant always talks in its own voice. Cues are short sounds, like a block connecting or entering a stack. With Hey Neon on, the microphone stays open to hear it, on this device only.

Downloads your program with your answers, to hand in to your teacher.

Show question blocks in the toolbox and edit them.

Learners may let the assistant do all steps of a change at once. Saved with the task.

If the voice engine can't load, learners may use the browser's speech recognition, which can send their voice to the browser's company. Saved with the task.

Export saves the task as learners first see it: answered questions become empty again. Import also opens a learner's saved work; Run then assesses it against the task tests.
